What you will improve

B2 requires more than knowing grammar rules. You need to use German actively.

Grammar

Konjunktiv II, passive voice, connectors, relative clauses and complex sentence structures.

Vocabulary

Build topic-based vocabulary and learn useful expressions for B2 communication.

Speaking

Explain opinions, discuss problems, give arguments and react naturally.

Writing

Structure formal texts, complaints, arguments and exam responses.

TELC DEUTSCH B2

Prepare for the exam

After building your German, switch from general learning to exam-specific practice. Learn the task structure and train each skill under time pressure.

  • Leseverstehen — Reading
  • Hörverstehen — Listening
  • Schriftlicher Ausdruck — Writing
  • Mündlicher Ausdruck — Speaking
